Accenture: Revenue Guidance vs Actual Results

Project goal
An analysis of Accenture plc's quarterly performance against its own published revenue guidance, using public earnings releases filed with the SEC. It covers guidance-vs-actual variance, the Consulting vs Managed Services revenue mix, and revenue per employee.

Key question
How accurately does Accenture forecast its own quarterly revenue?

Data
- Source: Accenture's quarterly earnings releases (Form 8-K exhibits on SEC EDGAR). Every figure is linked to its source document in the spreadsheet.
- Revenue figures are converted from thousands of US dollars to billions.
- Headcount is the approximate figure Accenture publishes, so revenue per employee is an estimate.

 Method
- Guidance midpoint = (low + high) / 2
- Variance = actual revenue − guidance midpoint
- Variance % = variance / midpoint

Progress

|---|---|---|---|---|
| FY26 Q4 | 18.68 | 17.75 – 18.40 | +3.3% | Above range |
